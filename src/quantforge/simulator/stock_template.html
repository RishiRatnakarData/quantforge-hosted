The mathematics underneath

Return = new portfolio value ÷ previous value − 1. Annualized volatility uses the sample standard deviation of daily returns multiplied by √252. Maximum drawdown finds the largest decline from a running wealth peak. These are past sample measures, not guaranteed future outcomes.

Our Python backend also includes historical and normal VaR, EWMA, Gaussian GARCH, two-state regimes, machine-learning volatility estimates, Monte Carlo, and portfolio optimization. Those advanced model results are not represented as empirical stock performance until run against a separately licensed, independently validated dataset.

Transparency

Where do these numbers come from?

Every published observation is source-labeled and fingerprinted. The simulator can also be generated locally from your own properly licensed historical data.

Historical game dataset

The demonstration replay is not a statistically valid stock-picking competition: the stock universe was chosen retrospectively, and adjusted price levels incorporate later corporate actions. We clearly separate observed historical backtests from simulated market examples.

Verification and limitations

  • Trade orders queued after each observed close, executed at the next observed open
  • Long-only, fractional shares, cash allowed
  • Explicit transaction costs on each fill
  • Benchmark has the same initial budget and entry cost
  • No future prices shown during replay
  • No taxes, slippage, interest on cash or borrowing
  • Original SEC filing-level audit pending for some valuation adjustments
