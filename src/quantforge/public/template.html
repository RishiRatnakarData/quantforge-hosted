How to read this

A 95% risk warning should be exceeded in about 5 out of every 100 observations over a suitable period. Both too many and too few exceptions can raise questions.

These are exploratory backtests of EUR reference-rate changes. They do not measure realized returns on a financed or executable trading strategy.
