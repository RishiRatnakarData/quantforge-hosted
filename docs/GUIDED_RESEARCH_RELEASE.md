# Guided investment research release

This assignment continues the deployed `4fc1a46` baseline on `codex/guided-investment-research`. Final source SHA, CI links, deployment IDs and live acceptance belong to the external release record generated after commit and publication; they must not be inferred from this document.

## Product changes and retained parity

Explore now has Financials, private Market history, Company briefing and private My research. News & Filings / News & Context, headline-tone graphics, NLP shortcuts and routine news requests are retired. Old news/context/sentiment/filings hashes migrate to supported Explore financials. No blank tab or CSS-only removal is used. SEC Company Facts URLs, fiscal ends, filing dates and accession records remain beside financial evidence.

The briefing integrates six research questions: understand the business, examine performance, distinguish valuation assumptions, investigate risk, compare alternatives and record/review a thesis. Facts are distinguished from assumptions and user-written beliefs. Business descriptions are withheld where structured data cannot establish them. The existing four-company SEC case study is linked directly from the research process; it remains calculated from published statements, with source ledger and CSV export, and works without GitHub access.

The thesis journal supports optional interest, evidence, concerns, assumptions, disconfirming evidence, open questions and a review date. It stores a bounded `QFTHESIS1|` JSON text record through the existing MFA-protected `/private/notes` endpoint. Existing plain-text and QF96 archive notes remain readable. Reviewing a thesis loads its beliefs and original evidence period alongside the current financial period and recorded-versus-current revenue, margin and FCF. Observation differences may reflect periods, coverage or restatements; they are not investment gains. Saving a revision appends a new dated note with an ID, revision number and parent timestamp. The previous text and timestamp remain intact. Ordinary note edits now also append dated records. No migration or new database is required. A full 100-note archive refuses new saves rather than dropping the oldest entry. Explicit user deletion remains available. Review dates are prompts, not automatic reminders.

Financials adds an interactive Balance sheet exhibit backed by the existing cached SEC Company Facts for the published issuer universe. It shows cash, assets, liabilities, equity, current assets/liabilities and separate current/noncurrent long-term debt components. Instant USD facts must match the fiscal end, be from 10-K/10-K/A, and have filing dates no later than the published issuer cutoff. Amendments are selected deterministically. Current ratio and liabilities/assets require positive denominators and matching source accessions. Debt components are only summed when both exist in the same accession; the result is explicitly not complete total debt. Negative equity and missing facts remain visible. Banks/insurers may lack a comparable classified balance sheet. Cash-flow consistency findings count positive covered annual proxies without attributing causes.

The company identity now shows extraction cutoff, covered fiscal period, latest included filing and observed market date when available. Snapshot dates are not claimed to be current filing completeness. Portfolio adds observed single-position/cash concentration interpretation when a complete authorized account valuation exists. Existing What If?, correlation/covariance, minimum variance and historical portfolio analysis remain unchanged.

All five sections and all Prediction/Risk/Valuation/Strategy Results/Evaluation/Method views remain. Chronological ML, baselines, calibration/Brier, drawdown/tails, uncertainty, DCF sensitivity, optimization, walk-forward costs and all broader offline engines remain. Public paths stay synthetic and explicitly labeled; private stock history is split-adjusted price-only and excludes dividend reinvestment. ETFs/funds are identified only where provider metadata supports the classification; expense ratios and look-through exposures are not invented.

## Archival NLP decision

`cloud/news_intelligence_v180.py`, its endpoint/security tests, pure teaching calculation and earlier research source remain for reproducibility. `news_context_v27.js` and headline-policy fixtures are no longer bundled. The current UI has no NLP discipline or worked-headline selector. The unused headline loader was removed from the browser request path. The archived authenticated endpoint remains MFA/owner protected for compatibility; it has not been made public. This experimental lexical work was retired for limited demonstrated decision value, not because its labels became investment predictions.

## Verification and source evidence

New Python fixtures exercise CIK identity, instant/duration separation, filing cutoff/amendment selection, missing values, invalid numbers, negative equity and non-construction of quarterly values. New JavaScript tests exercise thesis encoding/ancestry, compatible ratios, missingness and concentration accounting. `tools/smoke_guided_research.py` tests Chromium/Firefox/WebKit at 320/390/768/1024/1440: old-route migration, news removal, health provenance, selected-company Compare, escaped thesis content, append-only revisions, simulated persistence after reload, archive capacity and mode isolation. All private writes are local fixtures. CI retains the earlier numerical/security/browser suites and adds these workflows; its browser timeout was extended to accommodate the added coverage, without bypassing checks.

Cached Microsoft balance-sheet facts were spot-checked against the [2026 annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312526323660/msft-20260630.htm). Health extraction uses only already available SEC records and the published cutoff, not a new data feed. The reproducible builder is `tools/build_health_snapshot.py`; the committed normalized artifact is `web/site/sec_health_snapshot.json`.

## Deliberate limits and publication

Quarterly/TTM cash flows require reliable duration/YTD reconstruction and restatement handling; they are deferred rather than inferred from annual or cumulative facts. Complete debt, per-share valuation bridges, verified fund costs/holdings and automated reminders are not claimed. The notes store holds at most 100 records, including revisions; no silent pruning occurs. Real owner note/trade/funding persistence remains a separate owner-controlled acceptance item. Automated fixtures cannot prove it.

The repository stays private. A separate employer source sample could be useful: a small sanitized package of SEC normalization, pure ratio/risk functions, mathematical tests, architecture and the public case-study ledger. It would exclude provider adapters, owner data, deployment configuration and credentials. Publishing that separate artifact is deferred for a distinct review; this assignment does not expose the repository.
