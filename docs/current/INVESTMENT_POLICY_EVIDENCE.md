# Investment research policy: evidence and performance boundaries

## What drives the current judgment

One policy (`QFDecision34.assess`, wrapping the retained Guide30) produces the primary and detailed report. Batch and individual acquisition supply the same input contract. Coverage is an independent field, not a favorable investment label or a second ranking system.

| Evidence | Supports | Does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dated SEC annual facts and same-filing balance-sheet facts | Reported growth, operating margin, cash generation, accounting health | Future earnings, full business narrative, price attractiveness |
| Derived annual ratios and dated market cap | Reproducible annual multiples under source/period guards | Verified TTM, diluted EPS P/E, intrinsic fair price |
| Observed market history, drawdown and held-name correlation | Loaded-window downside and co-movement | A future loss bound, complete fund look-through or future diversification |
| Chronological L2 five-session classifier | Held-out direction diagnostics, Brier/baseline comparison, deterioration | Multi-year entry/exit authority, expected returns or profitability |
| Cash-flow scenario and discount/growth sensitivity | Consequences of explicit assumptions | Validated full FCFF enterprise-to-diluted-equity value |
| User-selected price/FCF, drawdown, weight, overlap and cash limits | Conditional constraint checks and hypothetical allocation impact | Inferred suitability, automatic orders or an objectively correct fair-value ceiling |
| Deterministic research heuristic | A traceable disposition and next question | Demonstrated economic alpha |

Coverage: Complete coverage, Partial coverage, Outdated evidence, Provider unavailable and Research failed. The last is an actual processing error with no defensible completed result. Unusable/mismatched response contracts are partial/incompatible; failed source requests are unavailable, without claiming a rate limit, provider verification or network diagnosis. Real 401/403 authorization failures still cancel and clear local Private evidence. Seven-day market-history/price research recency and 550-day annual/share review windows differ from the unchanged transaction quote policies (900 seconds stocks/funds; 300 seconds crypto).

Rule precedence: invalid conditions; explicit constraint breach; missing required coverage/selected-condition inputs; short-horizon deterioration only outside the long-term workflow; financial comparability; financial cautions; transaction observation freshness; conditional entry. No chosen rules implies Investigate, never an inferred entry. Favorable fundamentals alone do not establish an attractive price. Long-horizon model deterioration remains visible and marks the model limited, but cannot independently prescribe a multi-year entry or exit. Full equity and TTM valuations remain withheld when necessary inputs are absent.

## Has this investment-selection policy demonstrated profitability?

**Not established.** Numerical correctness, browser acceptance and a classifier's held-out Brier improvement are separate from net economic performance of the full research policy.

The current SEC snapshot contains selected latest annual facts and source dates, not a complete point-in-time history of the screen's original eligible universe, filing revisions, listing changes and delisted securities. Applying today's passing names to old prices would introduce selection/survivorship bias. Reconstructing decisions from later amended values would introduce look-ahead bias. Therefore this release does not publish a market-wide or multi-period performance claim for the shortlist/assessment policy.

Retained valid tools: chronological price-model evaluation, constrained optimizer, rolling allocation versus same-window equal-weight benchmark, fee/cost and omitted-window sensitivities, filing/revision coverage and historical replay with validated independent archival inputs. Those evaluate their named scoped methods; they are not evidence that the current financial screen beats passive investing. Official FF3 and independent filing-archive engines/parsers/tests remain available offline; hosted upload controls and instructions are removed.

Missing for defensible policy evaluation: historical as-of eligible universe with delistings; original filing/revision availability and matching balance/capital inputs; dated decision conditions; aligned adjusted prices and corporate actions; a fixed baseline/passive benchmark; realistic executable prices, fees, spread/slippage, turnover and cash handling. Only complete common historical cohorts may support returns, drawdown, risk-adjusted comparisons, period stability and selection-rule sensitivity. Withholding biased results is intentional.

## Optional forward paper protocol (offline; no owner records or new infrastructure)

Use the supplied `docs/current/FORWARD_PAPER_TEMPLATE.md` locally. Start a row only on or after the actual observation date; never backdate a current assessment. Freeze the policy version, eligible universe/screen/conditions, evidence dates, coverage and disposition, chosen horizon, hypothetical weights/cash, cost assumptions and benchmark before observing outcomes. Do not put credentials or private notes into a shared file. Manually record a new dated row; no hosted upload, persistence, monitoring or transaction is added.

At the prespecified horizon, obtain compatible adjusted prices/corporate actions for the complete frozen cohort and benchmarks, including failures and delistings. Preserve unavailable outcomes rather than dropping losing/missing names. Compare net hypothetical returns, drawdown and turnover with a financial-screen-only equal-weight baseline and passive benchmark on the same dates; report cost sensitivity, multiple periods and sample size. Risk-adjusted statistics need enough observations and explicit annualization. An unfilled template is not an evaluation and hypothetical returns are not owner returns. Until such evidence exists the economic-performance verdict remains inconclusive.
