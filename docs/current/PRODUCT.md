# Current Public and Private product

QuantForge helps a researcher move from a business question to traceable financial evidence, model evaluation and an explicitly hypothetical portfolio decision. It demonstrates statistical software engineering; it does not promise profitable investment selection.

The shared interface keeps five Public destinations: Explore, Compare, Models, Portfolio and Methods. The grayscale-first Home introduces three steps: understand the business, compare evidence and test assumptions. A shared full-name/ticker picker carries company context across sections. Public SEC facts are real reported statements; illustrative market paths and accounts remain visibly synthetic.

| Destination | Preserved depth |
| --- | --- |
| Explore | SEC annual trends, balance-sheet facts, accession provenance, guided research, individual assessment and private thesis history |
| Compare | Two to five names; common-date paths, financial contrasts, distributions, correlations, sortable evidence and safe CSV |
| Models | Prediction, Risk, Valuation and Strategy Testing; Results, Evaluation and Method explanation levels; interactive histories, sensitivity and mathematical notation |
| Portfolio | Saved virtual account, dated marks, contribution-aware bookkeeping, scenario weights, covariance, optimization and chronological experiments |
| Methods | Data lineage, worked examples, statistics, ML, optimization and engineering explanations linked to real source and tests |

Private opens My Briefing. Research an Investment opens existing company tools, and My Portfolio opens saved virtual records. The original advanced sections remain accessible. No additional account, fund flow or background monitoring service is created.

My Briefing reports saved positions and research records, latest thesis review dates and concentration breaches only against an explicitly chosen boundary and available dated marks. It does not infer risk tolerance, reconstruct missing returns or claim continuous surveillance. Missing marks lead to Portfolio; missing rules lead to optional research conditions.

The financial shortlist contains at most five principal candidates from the exact supported SEC/provider intersection. It requires nonnegative annual revenue growth by default, positive operating profit and cash-flow proxy, compatible source lineage, a consistent revenue concept and a disclosed 550-day financial review policy. Negative reported equity or margins above 100% require separate investigation. Users can change the growth filter and sort by one disclosed financial quantity; there is no hidden weighted investment score. Zero candidates is valid.

These are financial-screen candidates, not completed investment recommendations. Each card identifies its fiscal period and outstanding market, valuation, downside, overlap and model work. Exact universe and exclusion reasons remain inspectable. Fund and crypto financials are never imputed from company statements.

Each individually calculated assessment gives one current proposal with supporting facts, opposing evidence, uncertainties, coverage and next questions. Optional single-name, historical drawdown, price/cash-flow, amount and correlation conditions are tab-local assumptions. Conditional entry requires explicit rules, sufficient matching evidence, available virtual cash and current transaction-quality observations. A rule breach can produce a conditional avoid-addition or exposure-review result. None prepares or executes a trade.

Observed annual ratios, estimates, scenarios and judgment are distinguished. The valuation display separates instant common shares from annual diluted weighted-average EPS shares; incomplete enterprise claims continue to withhold a full equity/per-share valuation. Model deterioration is described only from adequate chronological held-out evidence; a missing diagnostic is not a positive signal.

Saved thesis revisions and review dates remain genuine persisted records. The briefing reads them without modifying earlier text. Live acceptance is read-only; numerical write and replay tests use isolated fixture accounts.

## Final company-study workflow

My Briefing orders at most three priorities, investment opportunities, a concise virtual portfolio overview and recent research. Financial filters and optional personal research assumptions are disclosures. The batch action accepts one to five shortlist names, defaulting to three, and runs one company at a time. Waiting, Loading evidence, Analyzing, Completed, Incomplete and Failed are distinct states. All-source failure is a failure; missing domains remain incomplete. A company failure does not erase other dated results.

Batch cards give the full company name/ticker, disposition, support, caution, financial/market dates and coverage. Open full research selects the requested name and refreshes its evidence. Its primary assessment shows at most three supporting and three opposing findings, a next question and invalidation conditions. Detailed evidence starts collapsed; public guide steps and the thesis form stay directly accessible. No second recommendation engine or navigation destination is added.

Changing assumptions, eligible universe, owner version, authentication or data mode clears session studies. Cancel prevents late responses from restoring them. Existing owner records are untouched. This is on-demand research, not continuous monitoring or a claim of profitable investment selection.
