## What works

- **Compare:** 2–5 company names reused from Explore, exact common-date close-price history, hover/keyboard indexed performance curves starting at 100, optional SPY benchmark only with matching dates, a sortable SEC annual financial table with fiscal/filing metadata and safe CSV export, return/volatility/drawdown/Expected Shortfall, distribution histograms, risk/return scatter and correlation heatmap.
- **Public/private data modes:** public price examples are deterministic **synthetic illustrations** and are never presented as genuine company returns. Reported financials remain the dated real SEC snapshot. The authenticated owner-only comparison uses the original nonbreaking `GET /api/private/history` 2–5-company contract, without exposing provider payloads in public assets. Unsupported or unavailable data are withheld. Optional benchmark errors never block SEC or other eligible history.
- **Portfolio:** retained sample virtual transaction simulator and accounting controls, new historical portfolio replay with explicit source dates, asset-weight sliders always summing to 100% including cash, HHI/effective positions, daily rebalanced hypothetical performance, volatility, downside VaR/ES, split-sample volatility stability, correlation display and an explicit constrained *in-sample* minimum-variance study. Same research basket reused from Compare, with a sensible 3-company illustrative default only if the user has not chosen at least two.
- **Owner-only ledger:** after existing MFA, an additional read-only view of existing private Supabase virtual holdings and cash uses `/api/private/state`; where **every** holding has a valid recent authenticated quote from the already-existing `/api/private/quotes`, it calculates an *indicative* marked value, net contributed virtual capital and cumulative paper P&L. This is **not** a time-weighted investment return. Missing/stale quotes withhold P&L, not the stored positions. No real trading or extra persistence endpoint was introduced.
- **Compatibility:** Release A stock-first Explore and model engines remain intact. The previously identified legacy Portfolio accordion replacement is integrated; the old sample trade and rebalance modules remain functional. Other legacy research panels and final UI cleanup remain deferred.

## Methods and assumptions

- The comparison engine uses exact intersections of dates without filling missing prices. Simple close-to-close returns, sample covariance, Pearson correlation, 252-session annual volatility and empirical **one-session** 95% VaR/ES are labeled as descriptive historical statistics, not modeled future outcomes. Price-only and split-adjusted conventions, missing dividends and provider feed coverage are disclosed. Mixed equity/crypto calendar annualization is not silently performed.
- The historical portfolio illustration assumes **constant target weights rebalanced daily, cash with zero interest, and no transaction costs, dividends, taxes or spreads**. It is not the reconstructed performance of a real or recorded virtual ledger. Histories are bounded to 31–1,000 shared closes and equity session conventions. Account cash flows do not enter returns.
- Minimum-variance optimization uses the aligned sample covariance and nonnegative per-company maximum weights (70%) with fixed cash; fitted performance is in-sample and NOT an untouched chronological test or investment advice. The feature never claims future volatility reduction.
- The approved Models overhaul and Method source atlas remain Release C. Larger visual unification/retirement and live signed-in acceptance remain the later consolidation pass; there are **no new paid vendors**.
