## Research integrity and feature boundary

- The public demo contains **reported SEC historical annual Company Facts**, alongside a **clearly marked synthetic close-price illustration**. The latter is not an actual company price, a backtest source, a forecast or a measure of issuer risk. Do not call its drawdown or returns real-company performance.
- Private source calls are never initiated until the existing authenticated browser workflow reports the MFA-protected owner state as ready. The backend independently enforces MFA before all private provider access. Changing the issuer invalidates completed findings and in-flight provider responses.
- Single-company stock closes are split-adjusted but exclude dividends. IEX-only fallback is a single-exchange source. **Candlesticks and volume are not shown** because full validated OHLCV is not present in the current research response.
- SEC annual revenue/net income/operating income are reported filing metrics; margin and annual growth are **derived**. Free cash flow is specifically an OCF-minus-cash-PPE-purchases *proxy*. It is not unlevered FCFF or a share-price valuation. Annual filings can have differing availability, later amendment and issuer ratio semantics.
- The preserved public ML exhibit uses the real L2 logistic numerical implementation on an intentionally generated fixture; it does not establish out-of-sample market predictability. Private headline tone/topic processing remains **TF-IDF lexical matching and dictionary/negation**, not LLM embeddings or a return forecast.
- Compare currently supports sourced annual-SEC issuer comparisons, rather than misleading early normalized market charts. Complete cross-company market analysis and Portfolio What If? remain **Release B**. Final unified Models/Methods and retirement of legacy controls remain **Release C**.
