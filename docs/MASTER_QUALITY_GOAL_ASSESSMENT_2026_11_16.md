## Private research desk

Implemented and exercised in controlled cases: integrated strengths/cautions/disagreements; valuation and financial-health eligibility; stale evidence warnings; historical downside; held-name overlap and marked weight; explicit insufficient evidence; concise Assessment with full Supporting evidence; safe quote-refusal context; fee-inclusive average-cost accounting, realized versus unrealized display and legacy uncertainty. Existing saved thesis/note flows, authentication, MFA, state protections and advanced engines remain in place.

Contrasting acceptance cases are reproducible in `web/tests/final_assessment_cases.test.cjs`: strong financials with demanding observed multiples; weak balance evidence despite momentum; volatile growth; diversified fund without issuer financial evidence; correlated actual holding; stale financial/market evidence; weak/disagreeing predictive model; malformed data. Cases assert coherent caution and missingness, not real-asset suitability. A ninth case verifies the crypto observation calendar. Six focused browser journeys verify synthesis, navigation and safeguards with labeled fixtures.

Partially achieved: this materially reduces calculation and evidence-reconciliation work, but it remains a bounded research tool. It cannot establish investment merit from incomplete filings, produce validated autonomous advice or promise a successful trade. Live recovered owner research was observed working read-only; candidate owner persistence, fresh MFA/session acceptance and accounting writes are not live-tested or claimed complete. They await approved publication and controlled acceptance.

## Remaining priorities and release limits

1. Obtain exact-candidate publication approval, run appropriate CI once, publish with rollback and perform source-revision/owner acceptance. Current local tests do not establish a successful future deployment.
2. Review and remove the unnecessary Vercel automation bypass under controlled credential administration; no bypass was used. Desktop interaction stopped at a URL-confidence policy boundary. Review native zoom when desktop access resumes.
3. Full diluted-share/enterprise-to-equity valuation, quarterly/TTM reconstruction and original-filing time-travel data remain missing. Do not turn the proxy DCF into a share-price target.
4. Fund holdings/fees/look-through and mixed-calendar multi-asset crypto Compare remain unsupported. No fund issuer ratios or diversification claims are manufactured.
5. Existing legacy basis cannot be reconstructed reliably from the unused field. Do not reset history or display invented realized gains. Percentage/time-weighted account performance needs sufficient recorded marks and external-flow timing.
6. Predictive uncertainty is not calibrated for an investment decision; sample sensitivity is not a future confidence interval. Backtests retain selection/survivorship, regime and price-only/dividend limitations.
7. No exhaustive human copy review, native zoom, screen-reader certification or independent investment validation is claimed. Monitoring review dates do not create scheduled alerts.

Judgment: Public is a substantially stronger, technically credible candidate career demonstration. Private provides substantial reproducible analysis and clearer synthesis, with important data and validation boundaries. Neither should be labeled “100% finished” while these limitations and candidate production acceptance remain open.
