# Integrated research and navigation consolidation

## Changed
- Reported SEC peer comparison: accession-backed four financial ratios across the full published issuer snapshot, excludes missing/stale facts, median-centered standardized distances, transparent coverage, no market forecasts or synthetic substitution.
- Private Decision Center: risk veto takes precedence over missingness, primary conclusion given visual priority, descriptive score remains neutral, reasons and blockers visible, research dates retained.
- Public and private page routing: browser history and hash support for deep links; private destinations require existing authenticated session, private selection remains in memory.
- Public DCF: adverse valuation model outcomes explicitly explained without altering arithmetic or clamping outputs.
- Private page location and navigation affordances consolidated; all advanced tools and privacy boundaries retained.

## Constraints and owner acceptance
- Public SEC snapshot is static. Inclusion of an issuer verifies **snapshot structure and accession metadata**, not independently validates every filing against the live SEC endpoint. SEC filing provenance should receive a separate live source-by-source audit.
- Public similarity is **not** a historically validated investment ranking and has no backtested returns.
- Private investment assessment remains a **heuristic aggregation** of existing evidence, not an independently trained predictive system. The full multi-method engines were not replaced or falsely claimed to have executed if unavailable.
- MFA, real private Alpaca quotes, manual portfolio scenarios, archive imports, saved notes, and live browser navigation need owner authenticated manual acceptance.
- No credentials, private holdings, orders, or ignored archive data are packaged.
